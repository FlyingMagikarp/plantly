# Gynura aurantiaca

## Care

Moisture: slightly-dry

Light: bright-indirect

Temperature: 18-27 °C

Minimum Temperature: 13 °C

## Seasons

Growth: year-round

Bloom: November-February

Dormancy: none

## Fertilizer

Growth: species-specific

Bloom: species-specific

Dormancy: none

## Notes

* Use a dilute fertilizer with relatively high potassium; avoid overfeeding.
* Pinch growing tips to maintain compact growth, and remove the unpleasant-smelling flower heads if desired.
