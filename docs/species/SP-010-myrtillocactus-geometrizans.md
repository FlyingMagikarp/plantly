# Myrtillocactus geometrizans

## Care

Moisture: dry

Light: direct

Temperature: 10-27 °C

Minimum Temperature: 10 °C

## Seasons

Growth: April-September

Bloom: unknown

Dormancy: November-February

## Fertilizer

Growth: species-specific

Bloom: species-specific

Dormancy: none

## Notes

* Use a very free-draining, gritty cactus substrate and allow it to dry between thorough waterings.
* Apply a low-nitrogen cactus fertilizer monthly during active growth; do not fertilize during winter rest.
* Keep the substrate almost or completely dry in winter, watering only enough to prevent severe shrivelling.
