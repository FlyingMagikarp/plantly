# Adenium obesum

## Care

Moisture: slightly-dry

Light: direct

Temperature: 24-35 °C

Minimum Temperature: 10 °C

## Seasons

Growth: April-October

Bloom: June-October

Dormancy: November-February

## Fertilizer

Growth: balanced

Bloom: balanced

Dormancy: none

## Notes

* Keep the fast-draining substrate dry during cool winter dormancy; cold, wet conditions promote root rot.
* The milky sap can irritate skin, and all parts are harmful if eaten.
