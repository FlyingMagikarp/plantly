# Olea europaea

## Care

Moisture: moist

Light: direct

Temperature: 10-30 °C

Minimum Temperature: 5 °C

## Seasons

Growth: March-October

Bloom: June-July

Dormancy: November-February

## Fertilizer

Growth: balanced

Bloom: balanced

Dormancy: none

## Notes

* Use a sharply draining, gritty substrate and never leave the container standing in water.
* Flowering generally requires about two months of cool winter conditions around 4-10 °C.
