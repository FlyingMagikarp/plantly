# Hoya Linearis

## Care

Moisture: slightly-dry

Light: bright-indirect

Temperature: 15-29 °C

Minimum Temperature: 10 °C

## Seasons

Growth: March-September

Bloom: August-October

Dormancy: November-February

## Fertilizer

Growth: balanced

Bloom: species-specific

Dormancy: none

## Notes

* During Blooming-Season, use either a bloom fertilizer or orchid fertilizer.
* Do not remove old flower spurs after blooming, as they may flower again.
