# Phalaenopsis hybrids

## Care

Moisture: slightly-dry

Light: bright-indirect

Temperature: 19-30 °C

Minimum Temperature: 16 °C

## Seasons

Growth: year-round

Bloom: January-April

Dormancy: none

## Fertilizer

Growth: species-specific

Bloom: species-specific

Dormancy: none

## Notes

* Grow in a porous orchid bark mix and water when the roots and medium are almost dry, without allowing the roots to dry completely.
* Keep water out of the crown to reduce the risk of rot.
* A temporary temperature reduction of about 5 °C can help initiate a new flower spike.
