# Rosa

## Care

Moisture: moist

Light: direct

Temperature: 16-24 °C

Minimum Temperature: 5 °C

## Seasons

Growth: March-October

Bloom: June-October

Dormancy: November-February

## Fertilizer

Growth: balanced

Bloom: bloom

Dormancy: none

## Notes

* This genus-level guide suits common temperate container roses; cultivar-specific hardiness, flowering pattern, and pruning guidance take precedence.
* Keep the substrate evenly moist but well drained, and do not allow a container to stand in water.
* Grow outdoors in direct sun where practical; indoor roses need the brightest available sunny window.
