# Tradescantia zebrina

## Care

Moisture: slightly-dry

Light: bright-indirect

Temperature: 18-24 °C

Minimum Temperature: 10 °C

## Seasons

Growth: year-round

Bloom: year-round

Dormancy: none

## Fertilizer

Growth: balanced

Bloom: balanced

Dormancy: none

## Notes

* Reduce watering and pause fertilizer when growth slows in winter.
* Pinch or trim trailing stems to maintain dense growth; the sap may irritate skin.
