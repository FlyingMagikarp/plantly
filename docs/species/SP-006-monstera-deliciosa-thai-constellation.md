# Monstera deliciosa 'Thai Constellation'

## Care

Moisture: slightly-dry

Light: bright-indirect

Temperature: 18-27 °C

Minimum Temperature: 10 °C

## Seasons

Growth: April-September

Bloom: unknown

Dormancy: none

## Fertilizer

Growth: balanced

Bloom: none

Dormancy: none

## Notes

* Use a loose, well-draining substrate and water when the upper few centimetres have dried; avoid leaving the roots in standing water.
* Protect the cream-variegated foliage from direct sunlight, which can scorch the pale areas.
