# Begonia maculata

## Care

Moisture: moist

Light: bright-indirect

Temperature: 15-29 °C

Minimum Temperature: 10 °C

## Seasons

Growth: year-round

Bloom: June-August

Dormancy: none

## Fertilizer

Growth: balanced

Bloom: balanced

Dormancy: none

## Notes

* Use a light, well-drained substrate and avoid waterlogging.
* Protect the foliage from hot or continuous direct sunlight.
